    How this list is built

    1. Coverage. 2027 port turnaround schedules for San Diego, Los Angeles (San Pedro), Long Beach, San Francisco and Seattle were swept top to bottom. Seattle has no in-window mainstream departures; Vancouver is out of scope (not U.S.).
    2. Line by line. Each row was read off its own per-sailing schedule-index page (date, duration, full port sequence, interior per-person snapshot) and matched to the official cruise-line voyage deep link before being added. The final pass (Aug 28, 2026) added 1 new sailing (Island Princess Panama Canal), finalized 4 rows, and removed 9 hallucinated rows. The verification log CSV lists every check.
    3. Flights. 2 adults, SFO ⇄ port, arriving the day before embarkation and returning the day after disembarkation. Basis: KAYAK route averages (SFO–SAN $156 pp RT · SFO–LAX $204 pp RT · open-jaw via Vancouver $285 pp), each row linking a dated Google Flights search. San Francisco departures need no flight.
    4. No padding, no guessing. Carnival Firenze sailings in the window were verified as Miami-based and excluded. Mid-curve port calls (e.g. Emerald Princess visits to SF/SD) were not counted as departures. Where a price is not publishable from a trusted index, the row says so.
